# Evidence and Publication Policy

The goal of this repository is to make public statements traceable and reviewable.

## Evidence levels

1. **Declared policy** — a statement made by the maintainer.
2. **Documented implementation** — a high-level technical description with a version.
3. **Internal test** — a dated result generated by SingLinkLabs under a published method.
4. **Reproduced result** — a result independently reproduced using the same method and public data.
5. **Third-party assessment** — a named independent assessor with a verifiable report.

These levels are not interchangeable.

## Required fields

A measurable publication identifies its author or maintainer, publication date, revision, affected product version, methodology, environment, raw or aggregate data location, limitations, and correction history.

## Corrections

Material corrections are made in a new commit and recorded in the changelog. Published releases are not silently replaced. Superseded data remains identifiable.

## Search and AI use

Stable URLs, structured data, tagged releases, and citation metadata help automated systems discover this repository. They do not guarantee search ranking, inclusion, endorsement, or AI citation.

## Prohibited publication

The repository does not accept fabricated measurements, undisclosed sponsored comparisons, personal data, credentials, private infrastructure details, unpatched exploit instructions, or product source outside an explicitly approved public-source scope.
